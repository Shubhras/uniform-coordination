KIREIZ SPACE Platform Architecture & Operations Flowchart Full Flow

Complete end-to-end user actions and administrative backend processes mapped together.

User Flow & Operations Client Side

End-user booking lifecycle, size/product configuration, and contract signing.

  • Browse & Select: Accesses tablecloths, napkins, and table coord sets categorized under type="table".
  • Custom Configuration: Configures item sizes, fabric type, colors, and rent quantities.
  • Checkout Calculator: Subtotal calculated as price * qty * days. Automatically applies 15% corporate discount for B2B users. Flat ¥1,500 shipping added. 10% tax added on pre-tax totals.
  • Contract Agreement: Standard rental agreement created via CloudSign requiring a valid electronic signature.
  • Cancellation Window: Cancellation permitted only until 5 days before the rental starts (e.g. >= 5 days remains).

Admin Console & Backend Operations Admin Side

System administration, inventory checks, custom B2B settings, and return/late-fee processing.

  • Product & Inventory Management: Admins upload table items, specify categories, and configure standard/corporate pricing tables.
  • B2B Special Conditions: Configure B2B discounts (15% by default) linked to active special conditions rules.
  • CloudSign Callback Webhook: Receives updates automatically from CloudSign. Triggers receipt status changes and initiates final order confirmation.
  • Returns Validation: Scanning returned items' RFID codes. Bypasses RFID scan for Napkins, enabling manual counts.
  • Late Returns & Grace Period Calculation: Overdue items calculated dynamically: First 3 days overdue are free (Grace Period). From day 4 overdue, late fee = price/day * quantity * late days (overdue - 3).
